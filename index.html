The opportunity

The adoption problem is not only the vehicle.

Users need trusted information on range, charging, cost, safety and financing. Fleets need route-level economics. Cities need charging visibility and energy-aware planning. ELDO E-MOVE AI treats these as one ecosystem.

The solution

ELDO E-MOVE AI

A modular digital layer connecting citizens, EV fleets, charging providers, renewable energy assets, financiers, training institutions and city partners.

1. E-Mobility AI Navigator

Ask: “Can this EV make my trip?” “Where should I charge?” “What will it cost?” “Which vehicle suits my duty cycle?”

2. SmartCharge

Charger discovery, compatibility, route planning, charging-time estimates and smart scheduling.

3. Solar Mobility Hubs

PV canopy + battery storage + smart charging, with e-2W charging/swapping where commercially suitable.

4. FleetSwitch AI

Compare ICE and EV duty cycles using energy, maintenance, utilisation and financing assumptions.

5. E-Mobility Passport

Awareness, training, safety, test rides and partner offers that can be measured from first contact to adoption.

Five-city replication

One platform, different operating priorities.

Eldoret — demonstration city: solar charging hubs, e-2W awareness, institutional fleet pilots and a TENP innovation/test lab.
Nairobi — metropolitan node: high-utilisation fleet analytics, charger interoperability and demand management.
Mombasa — coastal node: last-mile delivery, public transport pilots and heat/corrosion-aware asset management.
Kisumu — regional node: e-2W and delivery fleets, solar charging and tourism/municipal mobility use cases.
Nakuru — corridor/logistics node: intercity charging, commuter use cases and logistics fleet transition.

Kenya context

Why now?

Kenya launched its National Electric Mobility Policy in February 2026. The Ministry has identified charging infrastructure, investment, skills and awareness as implementation priorities. Kenya Power has announced EV charger deployment that includes Eldoret, while the World Bank's Kenya Energy Compact identifies Eldoret, Nakuru, Kisumu and Mombasa in an initial charging-station programme.

Sources: Ministry of Roads & Transport — National Electric Mobility Policy launch; Kenya Power — 45 EV chargers; World Bank — Kenya National Energy Compact.
